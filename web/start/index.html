LowSight
Meta Ray-Ban Display · Junction hackathon

Low blood sugar warnings that keep your eyes on the road.

For drivers with type 1 diabetes. LowSight forecasts a falling blood sugar and warns you through your glasses, before it becomes dangerous.

▶ Open the simulator Source on GitHub
Try it in one minute
  1. Open the simulator and turn your sound on.
  2. Click “Glucose drops while driving”.
  3. Watch the glasses view: an early warning, then PULL OVER, spoken aloud. Press Acknowledge, like a pinch on the glasses.
The phone senses the trip

Speed tells it you're in a moving car. It speaks every alert.

The glasses confirm the driver

A first-person seat check: driver or passenger.

Audio warns you, eyes up

“Low in 20 minutes, plan a stop”, then “Pull over”.

Good to know

Validated on real hardware. The glasses app ran on Meta Ray-Ban Display, and alert speech from the phone played through the glasses' speakers.
Your own demo. The simulator gives each visitor a private demo with a 4-character code, so several people can try it at once. Enter the code on the phone page to hear your demo's alerts on your phone.
First load may be slow. This site runs on Render's free plan, which sleeps when idle. If a page hangs, wait about a minute and reload.
No camera vision on this hosted site. The free Render plan's size limits can't fit the vision model; the camera seat check works when the project runs locally.
Simulated data. Blood sugar comes from replayed test scenarios. Production would connect to a CGM (Dexcom real-time partnership or Nightscout).
Not medical advice. LowSight is decision support. It never suggests insulin or carbohydrate doses and never says it's safe to drive.
