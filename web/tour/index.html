The phone senses the trip, the glasses confirm the driver, and audio warns them, eyes up.

  • Early: it forecasts a low up to 30 minutes ahead, not just when it's already happening.
  • Eyes on the road: spoken through the glasses, acknowledged with a pinch or by voice.
  • Knows who's driving: a first-person seat check, so passengers aren't told to pull over.
